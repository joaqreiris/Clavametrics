Posted to each player ~30 min after the final whistle. CR-10 Borg scale. Combined with session duration gives us sRPE = RPE × min — the daily load proxy that feeds the Load Monitor and ACWR.

Scale used

CR-10 Borg · adapted

0 rest · 1-2 very easy · 3-4 moderate · 5-6 hard · 7-8 very hard · 9-10 maximal. Each row carries a verbal anchor in the player's language.

When & how

Auto-sent 30 min after session ends. Player picks one number, optionally tags body region + note. Total time on form: ~25 s.

Localized

Anchors auto-translate EN ↔ ES per club preference. Designed for one-handed phone use during cool-down or on the bus.

Two states shown

Left: in-progress form with score 7 picked. Right: confirmation with sRPE result + 7-day history.
