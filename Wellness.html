Design notes

Two states shown

Form (left) — what players see most days. Success (right) — confirmation with 7-day streak and history.

Mobile-first, no app

Designed to work on any phone via a tokenized URL — no installs, no auth friction. Survives offline submission & auto-retries.

Localized

The form auto-translates EN ↔ ES at the SMS layer per club preference. Tap a number, tap submit — done.
